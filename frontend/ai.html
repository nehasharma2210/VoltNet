How It Works

Our AI-powered GNN model analyzes power grid parameters to optimize energy distribution:

  • Renewable Percentage: Percentage of renewable energy in the grid (0-100%)
  • Battery SOC: Battery State of Charge (0-100%)
  • Load Factor: Grid load factor (0-200%)
  • Baseline Index: Select baseline scenario index

The model predicts voltage profiles, power flows, curtailment, and optimal battery scheduling for the power grid.
